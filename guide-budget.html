Best Mechanical Keyboards Under $50

The sub-$50 market has exploded with great options. While you'll make some compromises (typically ABS keycaps, basic switches), many boards at this price now offer features that were premium just a few years ago.

What to Expect

  • ✅ Hot-swappable PCBs on select models
  • ✅ RGB backlighting (usually per-key)
  • ✅ USB-C connectivity
  • ❌ Usually ABS keycaps (smooth, may develop shine)
  • ❌ Basic stock switches (often Gateron Red clones)

Top Picks Under $50

Redragon K668 / K671

~$30-40 • TKL • Hot-Swappable • RGB

Excellent value with hot-swap, good build quality, and customizable RGB. The K668 is full-size, K671 is TKL. Pre-lubed switches are decent for gaming.

~$20 • Full Size • Hot-Swappable • RGB

The budget king at ~$20. Metal panel, detachable USB, hot-swappable, and surprisingly solid for the price. Great first mechanical keyboard.

~$27 • 60% • RGB • Compact

60% layout with box switches and RGB. Great for beginners who want to try compact keyboards without breaking the bank.

Best Mechanical Keyboards Under $100

This is the sweet spot for most buyers. Boards under $100 typically offer PBT keycaps, better switches, wireless connectivity, and improved build quality.

What to Expect

  • ✅ PBT keycaps on most models
  • ✅ Hot-swappable (on most boards)
  • ✅ Tri-mode wireless (Bluetooth + 2.4GHz + USB-C)
  • ✅ Better switch options (Gateron, Kailh, custom)
  • ✅ Gasket mounting on select models

Top Picks Under $100

🔥

AULA F99 / F99 Pro

~$69 • 98-key • Tri-mode • PBT • Gasket

The most popular budget board. Tri-mode connectivity, PBT keycaps, gasket structure, and excellent typing feel. Pre-lubed switches are smooth out of the box.

🎯

~$69 • 75% • Tri-mode • PBT • Gasket

Compact 75% layout with all the premium features: gasket mount, PBT keycaps, tri-mode wireless, and 4000mAh battery. Perfect for those who want portability.

~$56 • 75% • Tri-mode • PBT • Gasket

Excellent value at ~$56. Gasket mount, IXPE + PET + silicone 5-layer padding for premium typing feel. Multi-device support (5 devices).

⌨️

~$60 • TKL • Hot-Swappable • 5 Macro Keys

TKL layout with 5 programmable macro keys, dedicated media controls, and volume wheel. Hot-swappable for easy switch customization.

Best Mechanical Keyboards Under $150

At this price, you enter enthusiast territory. Boards offer premium materials, exclusive switches, aluminum frames, and advanced features.

What to Expect

  • ✅ Aluminum frames on select models
  • ✅ Premium switches (POM, custom linear, etc.)
  • ✅ OLED screens / knobs / advanced features
  • ✅ Superior build quality and acoustics
  • ✅ Premium PBT or dye-sub keycaps

Top Picks Under $150

🎮

ASUS quality with tri-mode wireless, 96% layout (compact but with numpad), ROG NX Snow switches, and multimedia controls. Build quality is exceptional.

Our #1 Recommendation

For most buyers, the AULA F99 or F75 Pro at ~$70 offers the best value in the market today. You get premium features (gasket mount, PBT, tri-mode wireless) at a price that was impossible just a few years ago.
