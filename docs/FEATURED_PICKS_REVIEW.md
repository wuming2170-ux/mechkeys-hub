# Featured Picks Review

Featured Picks require regular editorial review. The page UI and card structure should remain fixed, while the recommended products should be reviewed and updated on the schedule below.

## Review Status

- **Keep**: Continue to feature the product.
- **Watch**: Keep the product for now, but prioritize it during the next review.
- **Replace**: Replace the product with a better-qualified recommendation.

## Review Frequency

| Page | Frequency |
| --- | --- |
| `best-value.html` | Monthly |
| `gaming.html` | Monthly |
| `silent.html` | Every 2 months |
| `hot-swap.html` | Every 2 months |
| `portable.html` | Every 2 months |
| Layout pages | Every 3 months |

## Review Checklist

During every review, confirm:

- The Amazon link works correctly.
- The product is available to ordinary consumers and is not a Bulk or enterprise SKU.
- The rating is credible and is not judged only by a `5/5` score.
- The review count is sufficient to support the rating.
- The product is still in stock.
- The brand is recognized and trusted by the target audience.
- The product genuinely fits its recommendation label.
- No clearly better alternative is currently available.

## Replacement Limit

Replace no more than 1–2 Featured Picks in a single review. Do not replace the entire set at once. This keeps editorial changes controlled and makes recommendation quality easier to assess over time.
