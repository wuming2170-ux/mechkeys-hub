# Product Recommendation Page Standard

Use `best-value.html` as the reference template for editorial product recommendation pages.

## Standard Structure

1. Hero / Intro
2. Quick Picks
3. Featured Picks
4. More Keyboards to Consider
5. How to Choose
6. FAQ
7. Related Links

## Featured Picks Cards

- Give each pick a clear editorial use case or award.
- Include the product image, product name, concise verdict, key strengths, and honest tradeoffs.
- Keep the Amazon CTA as `Check Price on Amazon →` and preserve the affiliate tag.
- Do not show fixed price promises or claim testing that was not performed.

## More Keyboards to Consider

- Use this section for credible alternatives that complement, rather than duplicate, the Featured Picks.
- Keep cards more compact than Featured Picks while retaining the product name, useful attributes, and Amazon CTA.
- Keep the list curated and relevant; do not turn it into an undifferentiated product dump.

## Product Quantities

- `best-value`: 6 Featured + 8–10 More
- use-case: 4–5 Featured + 8 More
- layout: 4 Featured + 8 More
- brand: no Featured section; 12–15 products maximum

## SEO Standard

- Write a specific, intent-aligned title.
- Use a positive meta description without fixed price or unsupported testing promises.
- Set the canonical URL to the page's production URL on `https://mechkeyshub.com`.
- Add FAQPage schema that exactly matches the visible FAQ.
- Include relevant related links for internal discovery.

## Does Not Apply To
