🎯 Budget Buying Guide

What to Look For

  • Hot-swap sockets - Replace switches without soldering
  • PBT keycaps - More durable than ABS, won't shine
  • USB-C - Modern connectivity, reversible
  • Gateron switches - Better than Cherry MX at same price

What to Avoid

  • Outemu switches - Cheap but inconsistent quality
  • No branding - Often indicates low quality
  • Micro-USB - Old standard, avoid
  • Proprietary software - May not work on all OS
