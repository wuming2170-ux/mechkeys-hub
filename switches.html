🔴 Mechanical Keyboard Switches Guide

The heart of your keyboard experience. Learn the difference between switch types.

🔴

Linear Switches

Red, Black, Yellow - Smooth all the way down

Linear switches provide a smooth, consistent keystroke from top to bottom with no tactile bump or audible click. They're the simplest type of mechanical switch.

Best For:

  • ✓ Gaming - Fast, unimpeded actuation
  • ✓ Fast typists - No bump to slow you down
  • ✓ Quiet environments - Quieter than clicky

Popular Linear Switches

Cherry MX Red 45g actuation
Gateron Yellow 50g actuation
Kailh Box Red 45g actuation
🟤

Tactile Switches

Brown - Subtle bump feedback

Tactile switches have a subtle bump midway through the keypress that lets you know when the switch actuates. No audible click.

Best For:

  • ✓ Typing - Clear feedback without distraction
  • ✓ Programming - Know exactly when keys register
  • ✓ Offices - Quieter than clicky, still tactile

Popular Tactile Switches

Cherry MX Brown 45g actuation
Gateron Brown 55g actuation
Kailh Box Brown 45g actuation
🔵

Clicky Switches

Blue, Green - Audible click + tactile bump

Clicky switches provide both a tactile bump and an audible click with each keystroke. The most "mechanical" feel.

Best For:

  • ✓ Satisfying typing - Classic mechanical feel
  • ✓ Learning to type - Clear audio feedback
  • ✓ Single-user environments - Loud but satisfying

Popular Clicky Switches

Cherry MX Blue 50g actuation
Kailh Box White 45g actuation
Outemu Blue 60g actuation
